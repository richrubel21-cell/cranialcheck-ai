What We Built

The First Zero-Hardware Neurological Screener

CranialCheck AI runs MediaPipe FaceLandmarker — 478 facial landmarks plus iris tracking — entirely in the browser via WebAssembly. No server. No cloud. No latency. Combined with Web Speech API for speech motor analysis, it delivers a complete neurological screen from any smartphone in under 90 seconds.

Every existing competitor requires dedicated hardware, a controlled clinical environment, or both. EyeBOX needs a headset. BrainScope needs EEG electrodes. neuroClues requires a clinic. CranialCheck AI requires nothing but a browser. That is not a feature difference — that is a category difference.

Our mission: Deploy neurological screening to every medic, clinician, and athletic trainer on the planet — without hardware barriers.

Technology

How CranialCheck AI Works

Four steps. No hardware. No downloads. No clinical setting required.

1
Open Browser
No download. No install. Open the URL on any smartphone, tablet, or laptop with a front-facing camera. Works on Chrome, Safari, Edge.
2
Auto-Calibrate
2-second automatic calibration locks your personal baseline. Head pose correction via facial_transformation_matrix activates. Exam is ready.
3
Run Exam
TRIAGE: 90 seconds, 6 tests. ACUTE: 4 minutes, 12 tests. EXTENDED: 8 minutes, 19 tests. Voice guides every step automatically.
4
Get Results
Per-CN risk flags, confidence score, exportable report, and longitudinal trend tracking vs personal baseline.
Clinical Evidence

Research-Validated Technology

Built on 46 peer-reviewed citations. Every threshold, every landmark index, every scoring formula is research-validated.

Military Validated

Talian et al. established normative oculomotor ranges for service members and veterans using FDA-recognized methods. CranialCheck AI calibrates to these military-specific norms.

Smartphone-Grade Accuracy

PupilScreen (PMC9283953) demonstrated 93% accuracy, 94% sensitivity on smartphone PLR — outperforming the NPi-200 dedicated pupillometer. iPhone video saccade ML reached 78% accuracy (PMC9632643).

HINTS Exam: 100% Sensitivity

HINTS exam (Head Impulse + Nystagmus + Test of Skew) achieves 100% sensitivity and 96% specificity for stroke in acute vestibular syndrome — more sensitive than early MRI. Fully implemented in CranialCheck AI.
